F-2023-081: IMU Desync

calendar_today 2023-10-14 person Author: J. Miller Status: Resolved

Summary

During high-speed lateral maneuvers in phase 3 flight testing of the DBF 2027 prototype, the primary and secondary Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) experienced a critical desynchronization event. This resulted in an uncommanded 15-degree roll anomaly and an immediate transition to the failsafe recovery mode. Telemetry analysis indicated a delta of >50ms between data streams before the logic gate triggered the abort sequence.

Platform: DBF 2027 Archangel
Subsystem: Flight Control (FCS)
Component: Pixhawk 6X (Hardware Rev B)
Firmware: ArduPilot v4.4.2-custom

Root Cause

Investigation revealed that the Pixhawk 6X internal vibration isolation was overwhelmed by specific harmonic frequencies generated during aggressive control surface deflections. These frequencies caused mechanical aliasing in the primary IMU (ICM-42688-P), leading to corrupted timestamp data being pushed to the EKF (Extended Kalman Filter). The secondary IMU remained stable, but the synchronization logic in the custom firmware stack failed to gracefully handle the corrupted primary timestamps, causing a software-level lockup of the synchronization thread.

Corrective Action

  • Implemented a software patch (PR #1402) in the ArduPilot fork to improve EKF timestamp validation and reject mechanically aliased data packets before they enter the sync logic.
  • Re-calibrated the failsafe transition thresholds to be less sensitive to transient desync events (<100ms) to prevent unnecessary aborts while maintaining safety margins.

Preventive Action

  • Updated the physical mounting hardware for the Pixhawk 6X, incorporating custom 3D-printed TPU dampers tuned specifically for the harmonic profile of the DBF 2027 airframe.
  • Revised the IMU Calibration SOP (DOC-772) to include mandatory harmonic sweep testing on the vibration table prior to flight integration.
