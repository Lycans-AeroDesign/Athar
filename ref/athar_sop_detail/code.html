SOP-MNT-12: Vibration Damping Assembly

Last Updated Oct 24, 2023
Author person M. Lee
Version v2.1
warning

Critical Safety Warning

Ensure all power sources are disconnected before proceeding with vibration damping assembly. Exposure to active servo mechanisms during installation can result in severe pinching hazards. Wear appropriate anti-static gloves (ESD-Safe) throughout the procedure.

This Standard Operating Procedure details the mandatory steps for installing the isolation mounting system on primary avionics modules. Proper execution is critical to isolate sensitive IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit) sensors from high-frequency frame vibrations during operational phases.

Required Tools & Materials

build
Torque Wrench 0.5 - 2.0 Nm
science
ISO-GEL-01 Damping Gel
hardware
M3 Hex Screws Titanium x4

Procedure Steps

1
Step 1

Surface Preparation

Clean the carbon fiber mounting plate using 99% isopropyl alcohol. Ensure completely dry before proceeding. Do not use industrial solvents as they may degrade the composite matrix.

info Allow exactly 120 seconds for full evaporation.
2
Step 2

Gel Pad Application

Apply one ISO-GEL-01 pad to each of the four corner standoffs. Press firmly using the standardized applicator tool to eliminate air bubbles between the pad and mounting surface.
